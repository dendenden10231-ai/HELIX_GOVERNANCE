Фраза про Маска и «3 голоса» добавлена именно в место, где в оригинале заканчивается призыв к явке. Это не пересказ и не спорная интерпретация: это изменение текста. [ECT-3] ([truthsocial.com](https://truthsocial.com/%40realDonaldTrump/117377141739477104))

### 3. Технические огрехи

В поддельной версии, описанной фактчекерами, встречается «Dummods» вместо «Dumocrats», а также иные искажения оформления и пунктуации. В оригинале есть ссылка `swampthevoteusa.com`, которую фальшивое изображение не воспроизводит как часть исходного поста. Это не единственное доказательство фальсификации, но дополнительный индикатор монтажа. [ECT-3] ([truthsocial.com](https://truthsocial.com/%40realDonaldTrump/117377141739477104))

### 4. Смешение трёх разных сюжетов

Скриншот соединяет:

1. реальный призыв Трампа голосовать на выборах 2026 года;
2. реальную старую реплику Трампа о Маске и «компьютерах для подсчёта голосов» на митинге 19 января 2025 года;
3. неподтверждённое, но крайне взрывное утверждение о тройном учёте голосов.

Первые два сюжета существуют отдельно. Третий не подтверждён и противоречит тексту первоисточника. [ECT-3] ([truthsocial.com](https://truthsocial.com/%40realDonaldTrump/117377141739477104))

### 5. Ложная точность

Формула «каждый голос считается за три» выглядит как конкретное признание механизма. Но в скриншоте нет ни описания механизма, ни документа, ни данных об избирательной системе, ни органа, который это якобы допустил. В данном случае громкость формулировки не заменяет доказательства.

# 1) Where the news comes from

### The real primary source on which the forgery is based

On October 3, 2026, Trump's account `@realDonaldTrump` posted on Truth Social about the need for a Republican victory in the midterm elections and the use of all legal voting methods. At the end, there is a link to the campaign website `swampthevoteusa.com`. Both Musk and the "three votes" are absent from the official text. [ECT-3] ([truthsocial.com](https://truthsocial.com/%40realDonaldTrump/117377141739477104))

### The earliest discovered publication of the fake version

According to Lead Stories, the fake screenshot was published by the account `@Road_trippn` on X on **October 4, 2026**, with the reaction "OK… WTF?". This is the **earliest discovered point**, not the proven primary source of the forgery. [ECT-2] ([yahoo.com](https://www.yahoo.com/news/politics/articles/fact-check-trump-did-not-011140166.html))

**What is known about the link to the source:**  
- it is not a document, not a press release, and not a White House statement;
- it is a publication by a private account on a social network;
- in the available description, there is no data that the author of the post presented the original file, a Truth Social archive, or confirmation from an official. [ECT-2] ([yahoo.com](https://www.yahoo.com/news/politics/articles/fact-check-trump-did-not-011140166.html))

### Who amplified it

The screenshot then circulated on X and became the subject of fact-checks. From the available materials, it follows that it was distributed, in particular, by the account `@ResisterChic`; however, a complete distribution map — who made the image, who reposted it first, and which large accounts provided the reach — cannot yet be reconstructed from open data. [ECT-2] ([news.meaww.com](https://news.meaww.com/fact-check-did-trump-thank-elon-musk-for-making-every-republican-vote-count-as-three))

The Lead Stories and Snopes fact-checks were published on October 5, 2026, and qualified the picture as a digital forgery of a real post. [ECT-3] ([yahoo.com](https://www.yahoo.com/news/politics/articles/fact-check-alleged-trump-post-210300786.html))

# 3) Where the stretches are

### 1. Substituting a document with a picture

The screenshot imitates the Truth Social interface, but by itself does not prove that the publication existed. The verifiable object is the primary URL of Trump's account — and it shows a different text. [ECT-3] ([truthsocial.com](https://truthsocial.com/%40realDonaldTrump/117377141739477104))

### 3. Technical flaws

In the fake version described by fact-checkers, "Dummods" is found instead of "Dumocrats", as well as other distortions of formatting and punctuation. ### 4. Mixing of three different storylines

The screenshot combines:

1. Trump's real call to vote in the 2026 elections;
2. Trump's real old remark about Musk and "vote-counting computers" at the rally on January 19, 2025;
3. an unconfirmed, but highly explosive claim about the triple counting of votes.

### 5. False precision

The formula "every vote counts as three" looks like a specific admission of a mechanism. But in the screenshot, there is no description of the mechanism, no document, no data on the electoral system, and no body that allegedly allowed this. In this case, the loudness of the wording does not replace evidence.
